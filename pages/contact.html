Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked

Whether it's your puppy's first visit or you're looking for something a little more custom, I'm happy to answer any questions before you book.

01 Booking & Scheduling

How do I book an appointment?

Text or call me directly at 289-213-9855, or use the Book Now button to reserve through the online portal.

How far in advance should I book?

I kindly insist on booking multiple appointments in advance—especially for Doodles, Poodles, and other high-maintenance coats. Consistent scheduling keeps your dog's coat in better condition between visits and ensures you always have a spot.

What is your cancellation policy?

I ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may be subject to a rescheduling fee. Life happens! Just let me know as early as you can.

02 Services & Policies

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in the Hamilton area and serve clients across the Niagara Region. Many clients also come from Toronto, St. Catharines, Niagara Falls, and Welland.

Do you groom all breeds?

I welcome all breeds, with one exception: I don't groom large dogs. For first-time visits with Poodles, Doodles, or other specialized breeds, I recommend a quick consultation before booking so we can set expectations and make sure it's a good fit.

Do you require vaccination records?

Yes. Up-to-date vaccination records (rabies, distemper, and any other relevant vaccines) are required for all new clients. If your dog has specific health conditions or special needs, please mention them when you reach out. I'll always do my best to accommodate.

Can I request a specific style or cut?

Absolutely. Customization is encouraged! That's what I'm here for. I'll always be honest with you if a requested style isn't in your dog's best interest, given their coat condition or breed, and I'll suggest alternatives that work.

What if my dog has never been groomed before?

First-time visits, especially for puppies, are handled with extra patience. Mini Grooms are available for puppy first-timers to ease them into the experience. I'd suggest reaching out before booking so we can talk through what to expect.

Still have questions?

Text or call me at 289-213-9855, it's the fastest way to get an answer!

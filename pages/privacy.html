Privacy

Privacy Policy

I respect your privacy and only collect the information necessary to provide a smooth, professional, and high-quality grooming experience.

Information I Collect

I may collect information when you contact me, request services, or provide details about your pet.

  • Your name and contact details
  • Your pet’s information
  • Messages, consultation notes, or booking details

How I Use Information

Your information is used to communicate with you, manage appointments, maintain service records, and provide grooming services.

I do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Platforms

If you contact me through third-party platforms such as Instagram, those services may collect and process information under their own privacy policies.

Retention

I retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, appointment history, service records, and administrative purposes.

Contact

If you have privacy-related questions or would like to request an update to your information, please contact me at thegroomer.ca@gmail.com or 289-213-9855.

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational or legal changes.
